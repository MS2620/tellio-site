Install Tellio

Tellio isn't on the App Store, so you install it yourself. Pick your device below. Each has a free way and an easier way, and Tellio tells you when a newer build is out.

Apple TV option A: Signulous

Signulous isn't part of Tellio; it's a separate paid service.

  1. 1

    Register your Apple TV

    On signulous.com, register a device and choose tvOS Code Signing. They show you how to find your Apple TV's ID (UDID). Registration usually takes under an hour.

  2. 2

    Check it's signing for your Apple TV

    In the Signulous dashboard, the bar at the top should say Signing for Apple TV. If it names another device, use Link Device to switch.

  3. 3

    Upload Tellio

    Download Tellio.ipa from the latest build on the releases page, then choose Upload App in Signulous. Leave every option unticked and the custom identifier empty.

  4. 4

    Install and set up

    Sign and install it from Signulous; it appears on your Apple TV. Open Tellio and follow the short setup. To update later, upload the new Tellio.ipa the same way and install it over the old one; your settings are kept.

Apple TV option B: atvloadly

Free, but needs a Linux machine at home that stays on.

What you need for atvloadly

  • An Apple TV on tvOS 18 or later, on your home network.
  • A Linux machine on the same network that runs Docker: a Linux PC, a Raspberry Pi, or a NAS. atvloadly doesn't run on Windows or Mac.
  • An Apple ID. Use a spare one, not your main account. A free Apple ID works.
  • Your IPTV login (Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist link) and/or your Stremio addon links, for after installing.
  1. 1

    Run atvloadly

    Follow the setup on atvloadly's GitHub page. In short: install and start avahi-daemon on the Linux machine, then run atvloadly in Docker. Its web page then opens on port 5533, at http://<your-machine>:5533.

    atvloadly on GitHub

  2. 2

    Pair your Apple TV

    On the Apple TV, go to Settings › Remotes and Devices › Remote App and Devices. Keep that screen open, then in atvloadly's web page pick your Apple TV and enter the code shown on the TV.

  3. 3

    Sign in with your Apple ID

    Enter the spare Apple ID in atvloadly. Apple sends a sign-in code to your iPhone or other trusted device, so have it nearby and approve it quickly.

  4. 4

    Download Tellio

    Download Tellio.ipa from the latest build on the releases page. Each build lists what changed.

  5. 5

    Install it

    In atvloadly, select your Apple TV, choose the Tellio.ipa you downloaded, and select Install. The upload reaches 100%, then the Apple TV installs it; this can take a minute or two.

  6. 6

    Open Tellio and set up

    Open Tellio from the Apple TV home screen and follow the short setup: your IPTV account, your addons and your teams. Each step can be skipped.

Or add Tellio's source

Sideloaders that read AltStore sources, such as atvloadly and FlareStore, can add this one. Tellio then installs from the list and each new build arrives as an update.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MS2620/tellio/altstore/altstore-tvos.json

It lists the 10 newest builds and is updated after each one is published.

iPhone & iPad

Tellio for iPhone and iPad is a separate app with its own builds. It shares the Apple TV app's settings and backups, so you can bring your Apple TV setup across with one code.

What you need for iPhone and iPad

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.
  • An Apple ID. A spare one is best. A free Apple ID works, but apps need signing again every 7 days.
  • A Windows PC or Mac for the first setup of SideStore, AltStore or Sideloadly.
  • Your IPTV login (Xtream Codes or an M3U playlist link) and/or your Stremio addon links, or a backup code from your Apple TV.

Option A: SideStore or AltStore

Free. Add Tellio's source once and every new build shows up as an update. SideStore refreshes apps on the phone itself; AltStore needs AltServer running on a computer on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Install SideStore or AltStore

    Follow the setup on sidestore.io or altstore.io. SideStore needs a computer once, to make a pairing file, and then uses the free LocalDevVPN app from the App Store to refresh apps.

  2. 2

    Turn on Developer Mode

    On the iPhone or iPad, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, turn it on and restart. Sideloaded apps won't open without it.

  3. 3

    Add Tellio's source

    In SideStore or AltStore, open Sources, add a source and paste this link:

    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MS2620/tellio-iOS/altstore/altstore.json

  4. 4

    Install Tellio

    Open the source, select Tellio and install it. With SideStore, make sure LocalDevVPN is connected first.

  5. 5

    Open Tellio and set up

    Follow the short setup, or go to Settings › Bring your Apple TV setup and enter the 8-character code from a backup on your Apple TV.

Using LiveContainer?

Add the same source in LiveContainer and install Tellio from it. It's handy if your free Apple ID has already reached its limit of 3 apps.

Option B: Sideloadly

Free, from a Windows PC or Mac with the iPhone or iPad connected by cable. Updates are installed the same way.

  1. 1

    Download Tellio

    Download Tellio.ipa from the latest build on the iPhone and iPad releases page. It's a different file from the Apple TV one.

  2. 2

    Install it with Sideloadly

    Install Sideloadly, connect and unlock the iPhone or iPad, drop Tellio.ipa onto Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID and select Start.

  3. 3

    Trust it and turn on Developer Mode

    On the device, go to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID. Then turn on Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode and restart.

  4. 4

    Open Tellio and set up

    Follow the short setup, or bring your Apple TV setup across with its backup code.

Updating

When a new build is out, Tellio shows a card on launch and Settings › About says so. If you added Tellio's source, update it from there. Otherwise download the new Tellio.ipa for your device and install it over the old one, the same way you installed it. Your settings are kept.

Good to know

  • With atvloadly: it re-signs Tellio automatically, so it keeps working. Leave the Linux machine running.
  • A free Apple ID can have 3 sideloaded apps working at once on each device.
  • After a tvOS update you may need to pair the Apple TV with atvloadly again.
  • To move to another Apple TV, or to your iPhone or iPad, use Settings › Backup & restore if you run a backup server.
  • On iPhone and iPad with a free Apple ID, Tellio needs signing again every 7 days. SideStore and AltStore do it for you; with Sideloadly, install it again.
  • App IDs count against your Apple ID, not the device: a free Apple ID can register 10 a week across all your devices, and they free up after 7 days.

Troubleshooting

The install stays at 100%

Wait two minutes, then check the Apple TV home screen. If it hasn't appeared, wake the Apple TV and install again. If it still sticks, delete Tellio from the Apple TV first.

atvloadly can't find my Apple TV

Make sure both are on the same network and the Apple TV is on the Remote App and Devices screen. atvloadly's page has more tips.

Apple ID sign-in fails

Approve the code on your trusted device quickly. Apple sometimes blocks new sign-ins; trying again later or using another spare Apple ID usually helps.

The Top Shelf is blank

Delete Tellio and install it again; the Top Shelf part needs a clean install. With Signulous, make sure Install as duplicate app was left unticked.

SideStore can't install or refresh

Open LocalDevVPN and make sure it's connected, and that the device is on Wi-Fi. If it still fails, make a new pairing file with SideStore's guide.

Tellio won't open on iPhone or iPad

If it worked before, it has probably passed its 7 days: refresh it in SideStore or AltStore, or install it again with Sideloadly. Your settings are kept. On a new install, check Developer Mode is on and, with Sideloadly, that your Apple ID is trusted under VPN & Device Management.

"Maximum number of app IDs"

A free Apple ID can register 10 app IDs a week. They free up 7 days after they were made; until then, use another spare Apple ID.

Something else

Use Settings › About › Send feedback in the app, or open an issue on GitHub for Apple TV or iPhone and iPad.
